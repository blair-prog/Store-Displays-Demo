    Single-Sided vs Double-Sided Gondola Shelving

    Single-Sided Units for Walls

    Single-sided and wall units line the perimeter of a store and hold product at the eye level of shoppers walking past. They are the right choice when a run sits against a wall.

    Double-Sided Units for Aisles

    Double-sided units stand freely and give you two shelving faces back to back. They build the aisles of a grocery, liquor or convenience store, and end units finish each run.

    Gondola Shelving for Grocery, Liquor and Convenience Stores

    Gondola shelving is the backbone of aisle layouts. See how it fits a supermarket, a liquor store or a pharmacy, or read Gondola Displays: The Retail Layout Secret Behind More Sales.

    Gondola FAQ

    Questions About Gondola Shelving

    How does gondola shelving pricing work?

    Today the gondola page is a quote form with units from $107. The redesign aims to show pricing on the page instead.

    What sizes does gondola shelving come in?

    Lengths of 30", 36" and 48", depths from 12" to 24" and heights from 48" to 96", plus 12" and 24" extensions.

    What is the difference between single- and double-sided gondola shelving?

    Single-sided units sit against a wall. Double-sided units stand freely and are used to build aisles.
