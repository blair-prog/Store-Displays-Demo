Store Displays for Apparel Stores

Build a Floor That Sells Clothes

Boutiques and apparel stores usually mix hanging space, folded product and a few focal displays. Racks give flexible hanging capacity, etageres combine hanging and folding, and mannequins put the outfit in front of the customer.

Apparel racks · Mannequins

Use the Walls Too

Slatwall and gridwall turn perimeter walls into display space for accessories, shoes and folded stacks, which frees the floor for browsing.

FAQ

Questions From Apparel Stores

What fixtures do I need to open a clothing boutique?

Most boutiques start with hanging racks, a few tables or etageres for folded product, a fitting room rack, mannequins for focal points and wall displays for accessories.

Can I mix racks and slatwall?

Yes. Many stores hang best sellers on racks and use slatwall hooks and shelves for add-on items.
