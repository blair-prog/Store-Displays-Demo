Frequently asked questions

Do you ship nationwide?

Sample answer. Per the kickoff, shipping is within the United States, excluding Alaska and Hawaii. Rates by weight, size and region are still being researched.

Can you help me plan my store layout?

Sample answer. A free layout request flow is planned. The client asked for free store layouts, in the way Displayarama offers them.

Do you build custom sizes and finishes?

Sample answer. AMKO lists Custom Manufacturing and Custom Showcases today. Real capabilities to be confirmed in the workbook.

How do I get bulk pricing?

Sample answer. Bulk and tiered pricing is handled by phone today and may be explored after launch, once performance data exists.

How long does delivery take?

Sample answer. Delivery times and freight rules to be confirmed with the client.

Do I need an account to see prices?

In this concept, no. Showing prices directly is one of the main goals of the redesign, replacing today's Login to see prices.
