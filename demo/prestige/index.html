Got questions?

Frequently asked questions

Where do you ship?

Store Displays ships within the United States (Alaska and Hawaii excluded per the kickoff). Order by 2 pm ET and Ground orders ship the same day; LTL orders ship the next day.

Can you help me plan my store layout?

Yes, in this concept. A free layout request flow is planned. The client asked for free store layouts, in the way Displayarama offers them.

Do you offer volume discounts?

The live site has a Volume Discount Pricing page. Tiered pricing may be explored after launch, once performance data exists.

Do you build custom showcases and millwork?

The live site has Custom Millwork & Showcases. Real capabilities to be confirmed.

How does gondola shelving pricing work?

Today the gondola page is a quote form with units from $107. The redesign aims to show pricing on the page instead.

Do I need an account to see prices?

No. Prices are shown on the page, which matches the live site and the client's goal of reducing request-for-quote drop-off.
