Store Displays®: three visual concepts

Static mockups for the redesign discussion, built from the SmartSites x Store Display kickoff call (13 Nov 2026) and the live storedisplays.com. Each concept uses the same content, drawn from the live storedisplays.com (real products, SKUs, prices, links and images) plus clearly marked samples, so the client compares layout and feel, not copy.

Read this first. These are not the real Shopify themes and not a build. They are HTML/CSS mockups modeled on each theme's public demo store (typography, colours, header, hero and section order), without the theme's code or photography. Product names, SKUs, listing prices, links and images come from the live site; the images are saved here at full resolution. Reviews, most copy, the industry pages and the gondola builder are SAMPLE or CONCEPT. The real build happens in Shopify once the client store exists.
