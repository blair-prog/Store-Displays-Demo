About the 4′ black standard extra vision display case

This showcase pairs a sleek black aluminum frame with tempered glass for maximum visibility, two rows of adjustable shelves and sliding doors that lock with a key. It is a popular choice for jewelry stores, smoke and vape shops and pharmacies.
